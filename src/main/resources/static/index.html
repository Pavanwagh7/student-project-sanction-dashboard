PLATFORM HIGHLIGHTS

Engineered for Academic Rigor & Integrity

ProJSetu replaces unorganized WhatsApp chats, lost emails, and physical paper sign-offs with an automated, synchronized web platform.

👥

Collaborative Team Lifecycle

Collision-resistant 8-character alphanumeric code generator with a strict 4-member limit and team leader approval workflow.

📄

PDF Synopsis & Storage Sync

Multipart document uploads with a 3-proposal quota. Deleting a proposal synchronizes both the database and the physical disk.

🛡️

Department Tenant Isolation

Coordinators are strictly sandboxed to view, manage, and assign only students and faculty from their own engineering department.

⚖️

Faculty Workload Transparency

Real-time visibility into the exact number of teams each faculty mentor is currently advising, preventing faculty over-allocation.

🔐

BCrypt Cryptographic Security

Encrypted password storage, password complexity guards, and dynamic HTTP servlet session management for protected routing.

🌱

Idempotent Seeding Engine

Built-in startup runner pre-provisions faculty mentors and department coordinators safely on boot without duplicate key collisions.

LIFECYCLE TIMELINE

From Team Formation to Official Sanction in 4 Steps

A transparent four-phase process ensuring smooth governance throughout the semester.

01

Form Capstone Team

Leader creates team, receives unique 8-character invite code, and approves member join requests up to the 4-student cap.

02

Submit PDF Proposals

Teams submit titles, structured abstracts, and official PDF synopsis files (maximum 3 proposals per group).

03

Guide Mentorship & Endorsement

Coordinator assigns a faculty guide. The mentor inspects synopses and endorses the winning proposal.

04

Official Department Sanction

Coordinator grants institutional sanction, approving the final project for semester presentation.

ABOUT PROJSETU

Bridging Academics & Technology at RCPIT

ProJSetu (Project Bridge) was designed and developed by Pavan Wagh at R. C. Patel Institute of Technology (RCPIT). Built with Java 17, Spring Boot 3.5, Spring Data JPA, MySQL 8.0, and modern web standards, it replaces manual spreadsheets and uncoordinated sign-offs with an automated, role-isolated capstone management engine.
