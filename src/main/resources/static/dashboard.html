Capstone Project Rules & Guidelines

Official departmental standards and workflows for semester project completion

Important
👥

1. Team Member Limits

Teams must strictly comprise 1 to 4 students from the same engineering department. Cross-department teams are prohibited.

📋

2. Proposal Quota

Each team is capped at maximum 3 project proposals. Ensure every submission includes a comprehensive title, abstract, and official PDF synopsis.

👨‍🏫

3. Guide Endorsement

Once a faculty mentor is allocated by the department coordinator, they inspect feasibility and endorse the winning proposal.

🏆

4. Official Sanction

The Department Coordinator evaluates endorsed projects and grants official sanction, finalizing the capstone for the semester.
